About Me

Hello! I am Brighton Munezi, a software engineering student at BYU-Idaho and the Head of the Science Department at Nyamauru High School. With a strong passion for blending education and technology, I specialize in building interactive educational Android applications and full-stack web platforms tailored to the ZIMSEC curriculum. My technical toolchain includes Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, React, and Node.js. Beyond coding and teaching, I am deeply involved in my community as the Secretary for Eastern Queens FC and the Vice Chairperson for the Better Environment Ambassadors in Dangamvura.

Mutare, Zimbabwe

Mutare, nestled in the mountainous Eastern Highlands of Zimbabwe, is a beautiful city known for its scenic landscapes and vibrant community spirit. Specifically based in the Dangamvura suburb, this close-knit area serves as a dynamic hub for grassroots initiatives, local youth sports leagues, and environmental conservation efforts. The region's rich culture and natural beauty provide an inspiring backdrop for both educational mentorship and active community leadership.
